Recipe to Waste Time

A simple recipe for accomplishing absolutely nothing.

Ingredients

1 phone

1 unfinished task

Several unnecessary tabs

No sense of urgency

Instructions

1. Think about the thing you are supposed to be doing.

2. Decide you will start in five minutes.

3. Open your phone to check one thing.

4. Forget why you opened your phone.

5. Scroll until you find something you do not care about.

6. Remember the task you were supposed to do.

7. Get a snack instead.

8. Return to your computer and open another tab.

9. Repeat until enough time has passed that starting the task feels pointless.

Result

Congratulations. You have successfully wasted your time.
